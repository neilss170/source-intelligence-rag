Northwind Logistics - Travel and Expense Policy (version 4, effective 1 January 2025)

Purpose and scope

This policy applies to every employee and contractor who travels on company business or incurs costs that the company will reimburse. Its aim is to make sure people travel safely and comfortably while spending the company's money as carefully as they would spend their own. Managers are responsible for checking that claims from their teams follow this policy before they approve them, and the finance team audits a random sample of approved claims every month.

Meals and daily allowance

When travelling overnight, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 45 EUR per day, which covers breakfast, lunch and dinner. Receipts are not needed for the daily allowance. If breakfast is included in the hotel rate, the allowance is reduced by 10 EUR for that day. Alcohol is not reimbursed, with one exception: at a client dinner, up to two alcoholic drinks per person may be claimed, and the names of the clients attending must be recorded on the claim.

Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 160 EUR per night, including taxes. In London, Paris and Zurich the cap is 220 EUR per night, because hotel prices in those cities are consistently higher. Bookings should be made through the company travel portal wherever possible, so that the company's negotiated rates apply and the travel team knows where people are in an emergency. Upgrades, minibar charges and in-room entertainment are personal costs.

Air and rail travel

Rail is preferred for journeys under four hours. For flights, economy class must be used for flights of six hours or less. Business class may be booked for flights longer than six hours, but only with written approval from a vice-president before booking. Tickets should be booked at least fourteen days in advance where the trip is known that far ahead, and changes that incur fees must be justified on the claim.

Private cars

Employees who use their own car for business journeys are reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre, which is intended to cover fuel, wear and insurance. Parking and tolls are reimbursed in addition, with receipts. Commuting between home and the employee's normal place of work is never reimbursable. Fines for parking or traffic offences are always the employee's personal responsibility.

Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted later than 30 days may be rejected, and claims submitted more than 90 days late will not be paid. Lost receipts must be replaced by a signed declaration explaining the expense, and no more than two such declarations are accepted per employee per year. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary after approval.
